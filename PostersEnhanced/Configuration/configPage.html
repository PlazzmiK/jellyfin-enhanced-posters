Scheduled Tasks

To apply or update posters across your library, run Update Enhanced Posters under Dashboard → Scheduled Tasks. Posters will only be re-rendered when settings or media information change.

To revert your posters back to pristine copies, run the Restore Original Posters scheduled task.
